## Abstract Claim Budget

The abstract may report the uniform three-seed principal result, the QMF comparison, the explicit Visual-only clean ranking, the low-light complementarity result, the matched reliability/fallback retraining effect, and the available-fault gate AUROC/AUPRC over three independently trained checkpoints. Complete missingness must be described separately as an availability rule. The class-memory probe may appear only as a separate control result and must conclude class alignment rather than a language-specific semantic advantage. Inference-only path masking and the post-hoc ensemble must not be presented as independently retrained main-model gains.
